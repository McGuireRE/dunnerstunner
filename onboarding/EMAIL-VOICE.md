# Emails from the offers system: Tim's voice and the review rule

Tim's decisions, 24 November 2026:

1. Every email is built as a draft first. Tim reviews and proofs it on the page, can edit it, then presses Send on the page.
   Nothing goes to a buyer, vendor or lawyer by itself.
2. When a signer is identified, Tim may choose "Known to the agent" or record an ID check. Neither is mandatory; it is a choice,
   and leaving it blank is allowed.
3. When an agreement is made, emails for all parties are constructed first (purchasers, vendors, and both lawyers by cc), with
   the agreed copy. Tim reviews and proofs them, then sends from the page.

## Voice

Warm, attentive and practical. Tim writes as the person holding the transaction together: congratulating, telling each party
exactly what happens next and who does it, and inviting them to keep him in the loop. Plain words, short paragraphs, NZ English,
no em dashes (use a colon or a full stop). Names as people prefer to be called (the nickname field), never a legal name in the
greeting when a preferred name exists.

The model is Tim's own post-agreement emails, which several lawyers have praised. Their structure and his recurring phrases:

### To purchasers (cc the purchasers' lawyer)

- Subject: "Congratulations! <address>" or "Your purchase agreement: <address>".
- "Dear <first names>," then "Congratulations on your agreement to purchase <address, linked>. It sure is a desirable property!"
  (or a fitting personal line, for example "It's a lovely spot and I'm really pleased for you!"). "The agreement is attached."
- "The contact details you have supplied me are:" their names, emails and phones as given.
- "This email is cc'd to your lawyer:" name, firm, email, phone. Then "He/She is the person who will guide you through the
  remaining stages of your property purchase. Keep me in the loop with any questions or progress."
- The other side's lawyer too (Tim's standing preference): "The vendors' lawyer is:" name, firm, email, phone.
- Each lawyer shows as a short block: name and firm, "Email:", "Phone:", and "Notices are served to:" when the email for service
  of notices on the agreement's contact page is a different address (Tim, 26 November 2026). The same address is shown once.
  Each lawyer is cc'd at their email and, when it differs, the notices address too.
- "The property marketing homepage with documents is here." (when a link exists; LIM linked where held).
- Heading "Roadmap":
  - "Date of agreement: <date>. The first working day is <weekday, date>." Note any public holiday in the period.
  - "Price: $<price>"
  - "Conditions: your action steps", numbered, one per condition in the agreement, each with its period and due date in bold
    and what to do now ("Forward the contract to your mortgage broker immediately...", "order the LIM now and ask for it to be
    processed urgently"). Where all fall due together, say so and urge getting started straight away. Only the conditions this
    agreement has.
  - Deposit: amount, who holds it and when it is paid. KiwiSaver: the whole deposit goes to the vendors' lawyer's trust account
    and cannot be released before settlement.
  - "Chattels to note:" anything "as is, where is" and anything expressly excluded.
  - "Pre-settlement inspection: around five working days before settlement, I'll attend the property with you..."
  - "Meeting with your lawyer: ... make sure you take evidence of ID and evidence of your current address."
  - "Possession and Settlement day: <weekday, date>: on this day you get the keys..." and that keys are released only once the
    vendors' lawyer confirms receipt of funds. "I'll endeavour to meet you at your new home on the day."
- Close: "It has been great working with you thus far, and I look forward to ensuring everything continues smoothly."
  "Faithfully," then "Tim".

### To vendors (cc the vendors' lawyer)

- "Hello <first names>," then "Congratulations on your agreement for the sale of <address, linked>. This email is cc'd to your
  lawyer, <name> of <firm>."
- "<He/She> will handle all legal notices regarding the agreement and ensure everything proceeds correctly. Keep us in the loop
  with any developments as soon as possible. That way <lawyer's first name> and I can be responsive and ensure you get the best
  possible outcome."
- "The agreement can be viewed and downloaded here and is also attached."
- While conditional: "Until the purchaser confirms the agreement, you have tasked me with marketing the property. The advertising
  will remain active and I will continue to discuss the property with potential purchasers."
- "The contact details I have for you are:" each vendor's name as signed, their email and phone.
- "Your lawyer:" and "The purchaser's lawyer is:" (or "purchasers'"), each with name, firm, email, phone and any different
  address for notices.
- Heading "Roadmap": "From here, the purchasers will complete the agreed conditions. In the meantime, ensure any bookings and plans
  pertaining to your move are only tentative "pencil" bookings. The property is not sold yet, but "under offer"." Then date of
  agreement, price, "Conditions: purchaser's action steps" (from the vendors' side), deposit, any vendor action items from further
  terms (for example work to finish before settlement, with a nudge to book it now), pre-settlement inspection, meeting with
  their lawyer, and settlement day ("on this day you hand over the keys and receive the balance of the settlement funds...").
- The same close.

### Privacy in these emails

Each party sees only their own contact details and both lawyers' details. Purchasers never see the vendors' personal emails or
phones, and vendors never see the purchasers'. Relab details and anyone's location never appear.

## Other emails (signing links, reopened notices)

Short and warm, in the same voice: who it is from, what to do, by when, and that they can call Tim on 027 562 7207 with any
question. Built as drafts Tim sends from the page, like everything else.

Open point for Tim: one-time signing codes are triggered by the signer at the moment of signing. Holding them for Tim to send
would leave a signer waiting, so for now they send automatically and contain only the code. Tim can change this.
